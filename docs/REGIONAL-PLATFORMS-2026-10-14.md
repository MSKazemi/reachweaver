## Market workflow and compliance

- **Mainland China:** Simplified-Chinese technical video on Bilibili; real answers on Zhihu, technical explainers on CSDN/Juejin; Xiaohongshu visual proof and WeChat Channels for original short videos. Treat WeChat Official Accounts separately from WeChat Channels. Gitee is an *actual maintained code mirror only*, never a presumed share channel. See official [WeChat public-platform portal](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/), [Douyin open platform](https://open.douyin.com/), and [Weibo open platform](https://open.weibo.com/). These are research starting points, not current approval evidence.
- **South Korea:** Naver Blog tutorials, moderator-reviewed Naver Cafe contributions, authorized KakaoTalk Channel updates. [Kakao Developers](https://developers.kakao.com/); official account messaging is not a public recommendation feed.
- **Taiwan and Thailand:** Dcard/Pantip discussion is manual and value-first; LINE Official Accounts target opted-in followers and do not generate organic impressions by default. LINE [Messaging API documentation](https://developers.line.biz/en/docs/messaging-api/overview/) describes an API but does not imply account access or ReachWeaver support.
- **Vietnam:** Local Vietnamese tutorials and opt-in Zalo Official Account messages. [Zalo OA portal](https://oa.zalo.me/); broadcast eligibility and constraints unverified.
- **Indonesia:** KASKUS community rules apply. Do not cross-post promotional copy into forums.
- **India:** Trial ShareChat/Moj for regional languages, subject to creator access and content eligibility; do not recommend TikTok for Indian acquisition while the government ban is in effect. [Indian government 2020 action](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1635206); recheck continued status prior to any operational decision.
- **Russia/CIS:** VK and Odnoklassniki are research candidates only. Check applicable sanctions, payment, privacy and cross-border provider restrictions with qualified review before commercial use.

## Scheduling and experiments

No confirmed native scheduler or direct-publishing API has been registered for the **new** networks. Prepare culturally adapted, attributable material as an *owner-reviewed offline plan*, publish through each provider's native UI only when permitted, and record native receipts/metrics yourself. The example 12:30 and 19:00 local times are untested hypotheses, never a universal best hour. Use an IANA audience timezone, pre-register an experiment, compare same-platform formats at equivalent post age, and keep missing statistics unknown.

Commands (read-only): `python3 -m reachweaver distribution-plan --region china --goal awareness`, `--region south_korea`, `--region taiwan`, `--region thailand`, `--region russia`. Regions are planning categories, not evidence of actual accounts.
