# Channel selection: meaningful reach versus raw audience size

**Reviewed: 2026-10-08.** Read this before recommending social platforms, allocating posting effort, or claiming that any network will deliver the most views. This is a **strategy reference**, not a publishing capability declaration. See the bundled [platform playbooks](platform-playbooks.md), [measurement guidance](measurement.md), and the full repository's [platform matrix](../../../../docs/PLATFORM-MATRIX.md) where available. A copied standalone skill may not include repository-level docs.

## Non-negotiable decision rule

Do **not** equate a platform's users, registered accounts, ad reach, or total video plays with the reachable audience of a new personal account. Optimize for the owner's intended audience and outcomes, not just a popularity table. Confirm brand, audience, region, language, content/production budget, account authorization and primary desired action before promising a plan; state safe assumptions if unavailable.

1. Choose **one primary outcome**: qualified professional inquiry, GitHub contributor, technical adopter, paper readership, website signup or broad consumer awareness. Keep raw impressions/views as diagnostic unless broad awareness is explicitly the goal.
2. Choose **one flagship medium** (e.g., real screen-recorded demo + useful canonical documentation), then adapt native pieces instead of identical spammy cross-posting.
3. Evaluate channels on audience fit (most important), discovery potential, proof/media fit, observable outcomes, production time and actual permission to publish. Mark unknown reach or conversion as **unknown**, not 0 or a fabricated score.
4. Pick **two main channels + one bounded experiment channel**. Re-evaluate on native evidence at 7 and 28 days. Do not stretch limited capacity over twelve profiles.
5. For HN, Reddit and private communities, **human participation and community rules outrank reach**. No automatic generated submissions, brigading or mass DM.

## Recommended channel allocation for engineering, AI, open source and research

**Priority indicates likely *qualified attention for this kind of work*, not global popularity.** Confirm actual audience and revise after measurement.

| Tier | Channel | Reason to prioritize | Initial creative asset | Delivery / consent note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | **LinkedIn (personal professional presence)** | Reach engineering peers, research collaborators, business decision makers and prospective clients | Verified field lesson, working demo, architecture trade-off, useful PDF document | Manual post in current ReachWeaver; access for member/company APIs not verified |
| A2 | **YouTube (search + Shorts)** | High overall discovery, evergreen searchable tutorials, proof-by-demo | 3–10-minute reproducible tutorial + derived original short clips | Script/storyboard only until video actually produced and authorized uploader verified |
| A3 | **Reddit (relevant subreddits)** | Questions and feedback from intent-rich niche practitioners | Non-promotional troubleshooting answer, reproducible demo or community-approved project review | Human-led; check each subreddit's rules, disclose affiliation, never mass-crosspost |
| A4 | **Hacker News / Show HN** | Concentrated technical early adopters; launch events can create short-lived discovery spikes | A finished, accessible, nontrivial interactive project with author-written explanation | **HUMAN-ONLY** writing and submission; HN disallows generated/AI-edited posts and automation |
| B1 | **Instagram Reels** | Very large, broader non-specialist discovery if visual storytelling works | Original 20–60-second on-screen before/after demonstration with subtitles | Requires real media and proper Meta permissions; current skill drafts only |
| B2 | **TikTok** | Large interest-based discovery beyond existing follower graph, especially short demos | Short visible transformation + searchable plain-language explanation | Current skill scripts only; audit/app rules may prohibit public API posts |
| B3 | **X and Threads** | Short-form public conversation about AI, open source, research and startups | Self-contained proof-led finding, conversational follow-up | Draft/manual; platform API scope and fees require recheck; Threads reported 500M MAU |
| B4 | **Bluesky / Mastodon** | Smaller but sometimes dense software and research communities | Concise reproducible note, honest question, source link | ReachWeaver text adapters mock-tested; real accounts not yet verified |
| B5 | **DEV Community, GitHub Discussions, relevant project forums** | Evergreen problem-solving + valuable developer discovery, not general mass exposure | Original technical tutorial with runnable steps and affiliation disclosure | Human editorial review and community rules; don't flood identical articles |
| C | **Facebook Groups, Pinterest, Snapchat** | Worth a bounded test only when an audience or media format specifically matches | Targeted community explanation, infographic or demo | Do not prioritize solely because MAU is large |
| Retain | **Discord, Telegram channels, WhatsApp groups, email newsletter** | Community depth, subscriber retention and conversion | Opt-in release notes, demos and high-signal updates | Mostly **retention/distribution**, not cold public discovery. Avoid unsolicited invites/DMs |

**Regional exception:** For Chinese-speaking users consider Bilibili (technical video), Zhihu (technical Q&A), WeChat public accounts (retention) and Xiaohongshu (visual discovery), only after validating localization, account/legal eligibility and platform rules. No automatic integration is currently claimed.

## Specialist exception: open-source contributors when Reddit/HN are constrained

If the primary goal is **GitHub-qualified visits, first-time issue authors and genuine PR contributors**, and Hacker News is flagged or subreddit self-promotion is restricted, read [open-source-growth.md](open-source-growth.md) first. Start with DEV technical posts, LinkedIn professional teaching, GitHub contributor onboarding and Bluesky FOSS discussions; test a real YouTube demo. These are goal-specific experiments, **not a claim that DEV ranks highest for all social reach**. In the full repository see `docs/OPEN-SOURCE-DEVELOPER-GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Goal-specific default choices

- **Consulting/B2B leads (e.g., AI infrastructure services):** LinkedIn + a proof-backed YouTube demo; small Reddit/DEV engagement experiment; call-to-action to a trustworthy project page.
- **Open-source adoption (e.g., Kubernetes, AI tooling, dictation software):** YouTube tutorial + relevant developer community; LinkedIn for professional trust; one community-compliant HN launch **only if the project is meaningfully usable**.
- **Research/paper impact:** LinkedIn technical note + a clear explainer on YouTube; specialist research communities where authors can answer questions directly.
- **Broad consumer attention:** YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels, test TikTok once actual video production is sustainable; compare qualified traffic not only loops.
- **Existing subscribers and community:** Keep Telegram/Discord/newsletter as opt-in updates while prioritizing other platforms for acquisition.

## Practical first 14 days (experiment template, not universal posting prescription)

1. **Day 0:** record per-account baseline and origin of metrics; collect source proof, audience questions, legal/brand approvals and current API restrictions.
2. **Week 1:** publish two different, genuine LinkedIn learning artifacts; one real YouTube demo (or a production-ready storyboard when video cannot be made); contribute one human-written answer to a relevant community only where rules permit.
3. **Week 2:** publish a distinct follow-up case study or honest limitation, and test one original 15–60-second video adaptation on a permitted short-form channel. Reserve time for responses to real questions.
4. Compare native metrics at equal age (24h and 7d). Record watch time / engaged views, qualified clicks with consented UTM where available, genuine GitHub referrals and collaborations. For YouTube, see the **August 24, 2026 views definition change** in [YouTube Analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281).
5. Continue / pivot / stop: define the decision beforehand, compare per hour of effort, acknowledge data gaps; never claim causal uplift from two organic posts.

## Output format for Claude

Return a transparent choice table: channel, target persona, reason, feasible asset, official rule/publishing status, measured baseline, goal KPI, owner effort, confidence (documented / extrapolation / hypothesis), source URL/date, and next test. Explicitly list **deprioritized** popular channels and explain why. Say if account-level analytics are unavailable; global MAU is never a substitute.
