# Strategy and creative research playbook

## Establish a brand source of truth

Use an independent brand card per identity with fields: mission, audience jobs-to-be-done, approved accounts, core proofs and canonical URLs, differentiated promise, tone/voice examples from owned writing, languages, forbidden claims/topics, content rights, CTA destination, and disclosure needs. Do not infer endorsements or achievements.

Suggested brands: personal research/engineering, NovaFabric, KubeIntellect, YazSes. Never quietly post project claims under a personal account unless the owner authorized it.

## Attention hypothesis framework

Every hypothesis must specify: intended platform, audience segment, objective, original evidence asset, hook style, expected mechanism, baseline, time window, measurement source, and stop/adjust criterion. Avoid fictitious algorithm knowledge. Qualitative confidence tags: VERIFIED (first-party/primary evidence), SUPPORTED (credible third-party evidence), HYPOTHESIS (to test).

Select opportunities by scoring 0-3 each: **audience pain**, **novel evidence**, **clarity/demo potential**, **timeliness**, **discussion value**, **contribution to a real goal**. Do not equate raw clickbait reach with success. Document rationale; do not present an arbitrary formula as scientific validation.

## Sources of authentic ideas

- GitHub release notes, merged PRs, reproducible demos, benchmarks with datasets and limitations.
- Accepted/published paper abstracts (state actual publication status), Q&A, learning experiences.
- Genuine questions asked by engineers and users; redact names and private facts.
- Public platform news, standards and technical incidents with primary-source verification.
- Contrasts, before/after stories and tutorials only where both sides are real and comparable.
- Trend opportunities only if they intersect with an existing audience need; no trend spam.

## Content engine

Turn one piece of evidence into: 1 short proof-led LinkedIn narrative, 1 compact technical thread outline, 1 Bluesky/Mastodon note, 1 short-form video script/shot list, 1 visual concept, and 1 FAQ/community answer where welcomed. Do not auto-publish the same copy on every platform. Localize tone and CTA per community, and prefer single topic per post.

Quality self-review: Would the reader gain something useful even without clicking? Does the first sentence state a real claim? Is proof visible? Is the CTA optional rather than manipulative? Would the author be comfortable publicly defending the post? Is the claim true in the latest source revision?

## Calendar discipline

Start with 2 weeks, 2-4 evidence-backed posts/week on the best-fit channels if resources permit; this is a planning heuristic, not an algorithmic requirement. Batch creation, but retain a buffer for fact-checking and replies. No automatic weekend/weekday formula without audience evidence.

## Community and reputation

Engage as a real person. Offer useful answers and thoughtful replies, not copy-paste promotion. Explicitly respect each subreddit/community's self-promotion rules. Never automate unsolicited private messages or retaliatory response. Escalate legal/medical/security/crisis topics to the human owner.
