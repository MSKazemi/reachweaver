## How a post travels

Every step runs on your own computer. The gold step is yours alone: nothing after it can happen without you.

<ol class="rw-journey">
  <li><strong>Find the angle</strong><p>Start from something real you made. ReachWeaver suggests who might care and which networks suit them.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Draft for each network</strong><p>A LinkedIn post is not a Bluesky post. Each draft fits its network's length, links and conventions.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Check it</strong><p>Length, links, placeholders, repeats of something you already posted, and claims that need a source.</p></li>
  <li class="rw-gate"><strong>You approve the exact text</strong><p>Approval is tied to that text and that account. Change one letter and it no longer counts.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Rehearse the send</strong><p>Every command is a dry run by default: it prints what would be sent and contacts no one.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Send, or post it yourself</strong><p>Live sending needs three deliberate switches. For networks without an adapter you get a ready package to post by hand.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Learn from real numbers</strong><p>Import your own results to see what worked for you. Hypotheses, never promises of reach.</p></li>
</ol>

## What it will never do

- Post without your approval of that exact text.
- Store your passwords. Credentials stay in your own environment and are read only at the moment of sending.
- Buy views, fake engagement, scrape, bypass CAPTCHAs or mass-message strangers.
- Write for Hacker News or Reddit. Those stay human-only.
- Resend a post when it is unsure whether the first attempt worked. It stops and asks you to look.

ReachWeaver has no account or server of its own and makes no AI model calls itself: inside Claude Code, the research and writing happen in your own Claude session. Read the [security checklist](/reachweaver/publishing/security-checklist/) and the [privacy notice](/reachweaver/project/privacy/).
